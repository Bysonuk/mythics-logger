mythics.gg Logger: privacy and licence

Please read this before installing. It is the same as the Privacy section of the app's README (https://github.com/Bysonuk/mythics-logger#privacy). The site's privacy policy is at https://mythics.gg/privacy/.


PRIVACY

You choose what to upload. Nothing is sent until you log in.

The app sends nothing until you log in with Battle.net through mythics.gg in your own browser. It connects to no networked system other than mythics.gg (https://mythics.gg), and GitHub for its own updates. It also asks GitHub (github.com, where this app's releases are published) whether there's a new version of the app: when it starts and every few hours, whether or not you're logged in. That request carries no player data, only the app's own version; a new version is installed only when you select Update now, and only if its signature checks out.

After you log in, it sends only what you choose:

- Live logging is off until you turn it on. Once you've logged in, the app asks "Upload your pulls live while you play?" (Yes / Not now). You can turn it off at any time: Settings > Live logging, or "Turn live logging off" in the tray icon's menu. Off, the app doesn't read your combat log as you play.
- Past logs go only when you choose them in the Backlog tab.
- The mythics.gg addon is installed only if you say yes to "Install the mythics.gg addon?", and kept up to date only while Settings > In-game addon > "Keep the addon up to date" is on. Then the app asks mythics.gg for the latest version (when it starts and every few hours) and downloads it when it's new, sending nothing but those requests. It writes only the addon's own folders in the game's Interface\AddOns folder, while the game isn't running: never another addon's, and never your saved settings (WTF).
- Archiving is off until you turn it on. With Settings > Archive > "Archive logs once uploaded", or Archive on a log in the Backlog tab, the app moves finished logs into Logs\MythicsLogsArchive as .zip files, on this computer only. It never archives the log the game is writing or one another program has open, and deletes each original only after checking its .zip. "Delete archived logs after" (off unless you choose 30, 60 or 90 days) deletes archives it made once they're that old.
- You choose who sees each upload (Public, Guild only or Private) in Settings > Visibility for new uploads, or on each upload in History, where you can also delete it.
- While live logging, once a pull of tonight's log has uploaded as Public, the app makes a live report link for that log: a page on mythics.gg that anyone with the link can open, showing the log's Public pulls only. The app shows it on the Live tab for you to copy; it doesn't send it anywhere. Stop sharing turns it off at once. A log uploaded as Guild only or Private gets no link.
- Log out (Settings > Account) and the app sends nothing more.

What it sends, and only as you choose:

- Combat log segments: the boss pulls and Mythic+ keys from World of Warcraft's combat log text files (WoWCombatLog*.txt in the game's Logs folder), compressed, each with the visibility you pick. A combat log records everyone near you: other players' names, realms, gear and what they cast.
- With each upload: the log file's name (never its folder, which can hold your Windows user name) and a hash of that name and the file's first time, the segment's start and end times, the boss or dungeon, its difficulty or key level, its size and SHA-256, a hash of the pull's player list (to ask first whether a raid member uploaded it already), the region, the visibility, and the app's version.
- Summaries: for past logs, by default, wipes other than each boss's best go as a summary only: the boss, when, how long, the boss's health at the end, and the player-list hash.

What it reads: only the combat log text files in the game's Logs folder, and where World of Warcraft is installed, to find that folder (the game's install path in the Windows registry, and the usual install folders). It never reads the game's memory or its other files, never changes game files or settings, and never controls the game, except the mythics.gg addon's own folders if you say yes, as above: then it reads the addon's version there, and the names of running programs, only to wait until the game is closed. It changes no combat log unless you archive logs, as above.

What it keeps on this computer: your sign-in, and the current log's live report link, in Windows Credential Manager, its settings and upload queue in its own folders, and a log file of its own that holds counts, offsets and error kinds, never a line from a combat log, a name or an address. It never sends anything to anyone but mythics.gg, besides asking GitHub for its own updates.

To uninstall: Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > mythics.gg Logger > Uninstall. The README's Uninstall section says what is left behind and how to remove it. Logs you archived stay in the game's Logs\MythicsLogsArchive folder: unzip any you want to keep, then delete the folder to remove them. The mythics.gg addon, if the app installed it, stays in Interface\AddOns: delete its Mythics and Mythics_ folders to remove it.
